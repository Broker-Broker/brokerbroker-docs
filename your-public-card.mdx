Your **card** is your public profile on BrokerBroker — a page buyers and other brokers can view and share, built to be found by search engines and AI assistants.

Your card is **created when you complete onboarding**. If you see **No card yet**, finish onboarding — or use **Create my card**.

## Edit your card

Open **My card**. You can set:

- **Headline & bio** — a one-line positioning statement shown under your name, then the longer piece. Write your own, or use **Auto-generate** for a first draft and edit it. Drafts are yours to review before saving.
- **Cover & photo** — upload your own, or pick a ready-made cover.
- **Specialisms, languages, and operating areas** — what you do and where.
- **Transactions closed** — a self-reported total.
- **Social links** — LinkedIn, Instagram.
- **Your handle** — the last part of your card's address. **Set a handle** or **Change handle** any time.

Changes save to your card. A **completion** meter shows what's left to add.

### Let clients message you

Turn on **Show WhatsApp on my card** and clients can message you straight from it. You'll need a phone number in **Settings** first — the option stays disabled until you add one.

## Publish

Two things must be filled in before a card will publish: **at least one specialism** and **at least one operating area**. You'll be told if either is missing.

Beyond that, publishing is open — you don't need to be verified first; an unverified card simply renders as unverified.

Once published, the status panel shows your **public card URL** (for example, `brokerbroker.app/uk/your-name`). Use **Copy link** or **Open public card** to view and share it. After later edits, **Re-publish** to push them live.

## Get verified

Submit a licence or credential under **Get verified**. After an admin reviews it, your card earns the **blue tick**. See [Vouches & trust](/vouches) for how verification differs from your Buddy Score.

## What buyers see

The public card shows your headline, bio, specialisms, areas, verification state, vouches you've chosen to display, and your track record. It's designed to be **crawlable and shareable**, so it works as a link you can drop anywhere.
