**Groups** — labelled **Groups** in the app (some links still use the older name _circles_) — are private groups of brokers. They let you share **pocket** listings — off-market properties visible only to people inside the group — instead of publishing to the whole network.

## Create a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Groups → New group">
    Give it a name and a colour so it's easy to recognise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Search for brokers and add them, or send invites to brokers who aren't on the platform yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a group

When you [launch a property](/properties) as **pocket**, you choose which group(s) can see it. Only members of those groups will match against it or find it — everyone else sees nothing.

<Note>
  Anything launched as **public** is visible to the whole network and doesn't need a group. Groups are only for pocket/off-market sharing.
</Note>

## Invite people

**Copy invite link** gives you a link to send however you like. You choose whether it expires or has **no expiry**, and you can **revoke** it later — anyone who hasn't used it yet then can't.

## Join a group

If another broker invites you, you'll get a join link or an invite in your [notifications](/notifications). Accept to become a member and start seeing that group's pocket listings.

## Managing a group

- **Remove a member** — they lose sight of the group's pocket listings.
- **Leave** a group you're in.
- **Delete** a group you own. The group and its sharing go with it, so the pocket listings you shared through it stop being visible to those brokers.

<Warning>
  Removing someone or deleting a group takes effect immediately on what they can see. Neither is undone by re-adding them to a new group — the sharing has to be set up again.
</Warning>
