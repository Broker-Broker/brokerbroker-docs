**BrokerBuddy** is your assistant inside BrokerBroker. Open it from the sidebar to chat: ask how something works, get a hand drafting a listing or a buyer search, or check on your pipeline.

## What it can help with

- **Answer questions** about using the platform.
- **Draft listings** — describe a property in plain language and it can turn that into a launch-ready draft.
- **Update a listing you already have** — tell it what changed and it drafts the edit.
- **Capture buyers** — describe what a client wants and it can set up the buyer search. Try *"Add a buyer wanting a 2-bed in Canary Wharf under £800k"*.
- **Summarise** what's happening across your properties, buyers, and deals.
- **Explain your matches** and answer questions grounded in your data and the BrokerBroker knowledge base — with references, and an honest "I don't know" when it isn't sure rather than a guess.

## One conversation, everywhere

BrokerBuddy is the same assistant across the **web portal, the mobile app, and WhatsApp**, and it shares one history — so a conversation you start on WhatsApp continues in the portal, and vice versa.

## Talk to it

Type, or **speak** — record instead of typing when your hands are busy.

## It remembers you

BrokerBuddy keeps a private, per-broker memory of durable facts — how you co-broke, the areas and property types you focus on, how you like drafts written — and brings the relevant ones into a conversation when they matter, so you don't have to re-explain yourself each time.

You can **reset the conversation** to start a clean thread. That clears the thread, not the memory: *BrokerBuddy still remembers what it learned*. Resetting is for when a conversation has wandered, not for making it forget you.

## Connect it on WhatsApp

Open **Settings**, add a phone number, then link it. Once linked, messages to the BrokerBroker number reach the same assistant with the same shared history — and match alerts can come the same way.

<Tip>
  Anything BrokerBuddy drafts is yours to review before it goes live. Confirm listings and buyer searches the same way you would if you'd typed them in.
</Tip>
