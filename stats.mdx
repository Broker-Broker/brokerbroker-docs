**Stats** brings together two lenses on your performance.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Engagement">
    How your [public card](/your-public-card) is doing — views and interactions over **this week**, the **last 7 days** or the **last 30 days**. Views are de-duplicated so repeat visits from the same person in a day don't inflate the numbers, giving you an honest reach figure (including how discoverable your card is to AI assistants and crawlers).

    If your card has no public listings on it yet, you'll be told — an empty card is the usual reason engagement is flat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Performance">
    Your deal activity: **active buyers**, **opportunities**, deals **interested** and **completed**, **transactions**, your **rating** and your **response rate** — plus how long you've been a **member**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Some figures need your **agency name** before they mean anything, and you'll be prompted to add it if it's missing. Set it under **Settings → Markets**, where each market carries the agency you operate under there.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Low engagement usually means an incomplete or unpublished card. If your numbers are flat, start with [Your public card](/your-public-card).
</Tip>
