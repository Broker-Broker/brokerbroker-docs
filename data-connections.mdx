title: "Data connections"
description: "Sync your listings and buyers from Loop CRM."
---

**Data connections** let you bring data in from an external CRM instead of adding it by hand. Today that's **Loop** (available in supported markets, currently the UK). Connecting Loop imports your **listings** and also feeds your **buyers** into matching.

Find it under **Settings → Data connections**.

## Connect Loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Data connections">
    If Loop is available for your market, you'll see it here. If you don't see it, your market isn't eligible yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Enter your Loop **API key** (required). You can optionally add your Loop **User ID** to scope which listings import. Use **Test connection** first if you want to confirm it before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    Your Loop listings sync into BrokerBroker. Syncing then runs **on a schedule** — you don't have to trigger it. Use **Sync now** when you want the latest immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A connection whose test fails can still be saved — you'll see **Connection saved — test failed, edit credentials to retry**. Nothing syncs until the credentials work, so fix them before assuming the import is running.
</Note>

## Checking it is working

The page shows where the connection stands: **Connected** or **Disconnected**, the **last successful sync**, the **next sync**, and how many **properties imported**.

If listings aren't appearing, this is the first place to look — a connection can read as saved while the last successful sync is empty.

## After syncing

Imported properties appear under [Properties](/properties) like any other listing. Review them, set your **Buyer Finder Split Fee**, and **launch** the ones you want on the network — importing doesn't launch them automatically.

Your Loop **buyers** sync too (privately — no personal details are stored) and are matched against other brokers' live listings. Those matches appear in **From your Loop CRM** under [Opportunities](/opportunities).

## Disconnect

Use **Disconnect** on the same page to stop syncing. Already-imported listings stay in your account.
